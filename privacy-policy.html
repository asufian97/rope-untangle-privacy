Rope Untangle Privacy Policy

Stillwave Studio · Effective 1 November 2026 · Version 1.1.0

Rope Untangle is a puzzle game that can be played offline. This version shows occasional advertising between levels when the device is online. It has no user accounts, game analytics or in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

The game saves completed-level best moves and times, star ratings, your selected level, unfinished campaign and daily puzzles, undo history, daily completion dates and medals, theme selection, tutorial completion and sound preference locally. Daily puzzles use the device's UTC date. Stillwave Studio does not receive this information.

Optional result sharing

If you press Share Result, the game creates an image in its local cache containing the game name, level or daily date, stars, moves, target and solve time. Android lets you choose an app and recipient. The game sends no image automatically. The app you choose handles the image according to its own privacy practices. The generated image contains no account name, support email correspondence or device identifier.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads load, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. They use this to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice from Privacy Choices in How to Play / Privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Data collection and permissions

The game itself does not collect or transmit personal data to Stillwave Studio; advertising data goes to Google as described above. The game does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or your photo library. Result sharing grants temporary read-only access to the generated image; it does not grant access to other game files.

Support

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request. Please do not include sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting sufianwp97dot@gmail.com.

Deleting local progress

Clear the game's app storage in Android settings to remove local progress and preferences. Android may also remove cached result images. Images already shared with another app must be managed in that app. Device backup and restore may be controlled separately by your operating system and account settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Stillwave Studio does not itself collect personal data from players, including children. Do not submit personal information through support if you are a child; ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

Future changes

If a future update changes advertising or other data processing, we will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures before releasing that update.
